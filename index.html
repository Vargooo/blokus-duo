Goal of the game

Put the maximum number of your squares on the board.

Rules

The first piece must cover the starting point (marked with a circle).

Each new piece must touch at least one piece of the same color on the corners of any square. No two sides of the same color can touch. Different colors can touch in any way.

The game ends when both players are blocked from placing any more pieces.
